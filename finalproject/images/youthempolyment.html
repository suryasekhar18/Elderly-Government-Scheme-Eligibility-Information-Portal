Empower: Initiatives for Youth Employment


Aatmanirbhar Bharat Rojgar Yojana (ABRY)


  • Aatmanirbhar Bharat Rojgar Yojana (ABRY) was launched on October, 2020

  • The Aatmanirbhar Bharat Rojgar Yojana (ABRY) is a government initiative launched in response to the economic challenges posed by the COVID-19 pandemic.

  • The scheme aims to incentivize job creation and promote employment opportunities, especially for sectors heavily impacted by the pandemic.

  • The primary objective of ABRY is to boost job creation by providing financial incentives to employers who hire new employees.

Eligibility


  • New employees must be hired between a specified period as outlined by the government, usually between October 1, 2020, and June 30, 2021, for the initial phase of the scheme.

  • Employees must have a Universal Account Number (UAN) and be eligible for EPF benefits.

  • Employers must be registered under the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) and comply with EPF regulations.

  • The scheme typically targets specific sectors that have been significantly impacted by the COVID-19 pandemic, such as hospitality, tourism, manufacturing, construction, and others.

Benefits


  • Employers who participate in ABRY receive financial incentives in the form of government contributions to the Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) accounts of eligible employees

  • The government contributes both the employer's and employee's share of EPF contributions (12% each) for new employees hired during the specified period.

  • Eligible employees benefit from increased contributions to their EPF accounts, which enhances their social security and retirement savings.

  • The scheme helps reduce unemployment and inequality, fostering social development and cohesion.

PMFBY: Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana


  • The Pradhan Mantri Rojgar Protsahan Yojana (PMRPY) was introduced by the Government of India on August 7, 2016.

  • The Pradhan Mantri Rojgar Protsahan Yojana (PMRPY) is a government scheme aimed at incentivizing employers for promoting employment generation.

  • The primary objective of PMRPY is to incentivize employers for increasing employment opportunities, especially in sectors that have high labor-intensive activities.

Eligibility


  • Employers must be registered with the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) and have a valid EPF registration number.

  • They must not have been covered under EPF earlier and must be filing ECR (Electronic Challan-cum-Return) on or after the scheme's commencement date.

  • Employees must be newly hired and previously unemployed.

  • They should have been registered with the EPFO on or after April 1, 2016.

  • The scheme is generally applicable to all sectors except the public sector.

Benefits


  • Employers receive financial incentives from the government for promoting employment generation.

  • The government contributes the employer's share of Employees' Pension Scheme (EPS) contributions (12% of wages) for eligible employees for a period of three years.

  • PMRPY helps reduce the cost of hiring new employees for employers. By covering a portion of the EPS contributions for eligible employees

  • Eligible employees benefit from enhanced social security coverage through contributions to the EPS. The scheme provides employees with access to pension benefits

PM-AASHA: Pradhan Mantri Annadata Aay Sanrakshan Abhiyan


  • The Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA) was introduced by the Government of India on August 25, 2005

  • The primary objective of MGNREGA is to generate wage employment opportunities for rural households, particularly during the lean agricultural season when employment opportunities are scarce.

  • MGNREGA seeks to promote rural development by creating productive and sustainable community assets such as water conservation structures, rural roads, irrigation facilities, and land development works

Eligibility


  • Eligible beneficiaries must be residents of rural areas in India. The Act primarily targets rural households and individuals who depend on agriculture and allied activities for their livelihood.

  • MGNREGA extends employment opportunities to rural households, not individuals. Therefore, households are the primary unit of eligibility.

  • The household must have adult members (aged 18 years or above) who are willing to participate in unskilled manual work provided under MGNREGA.

  • To avail of employment under MGNREGA, eligible households must possess a Job Card issued by the local Gram Panchayat. The Job Card serves as proof of registration and eligibility for MGNREGA work.

Benefits


  • MGNREGA provides employment opportunities to rural households, ensuring livelihood security for vulnerable sections of society, particularly during the lean agricultural season when employment opportunities are scarce.

  • This provides a source of income to rural households, helping them meet their basic needs and improve their standard of living.

  • MGNREGA emphasizes the participation of women in the workforce and ensures that at least one-third of the beneficiaries are women

  • By promoting eco-friendly practices and natural resource management, the Act supports environmental conservation and resilience against climate change.

Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Rojgar Abhiyaan (PMGKRA)


  • The Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Rojgar Abhiyaan (PMGKRA) was launched by the Government of India on June 20, 2020.

  • The primary objective of PMGKRA is to generate employment opportunities for migrant workers who returned to their native villages due to the COVID-19 pandemic

  • By providing employment through various rural development projects, the scheme aims to support livelihoods and alleviate the economic distress faced by migrant workers.

Eligibility


  • Beneficiaries must be migrant workers who returned to their native villages during the COVID-19 pandemic.

  • Beneficiaries should be residents of rural areas where PMGKRA projects are implemented.

  • There may not be strict age criteria for beneficiaries; however, the focus is typically on adults who are of working age and capable of participating in manual labor or other relevant activities

  • Beneficiaries must comply with the guidelines and procedures laid down by the implementing authorities for participation in PMGKRA projects.

Benefits


  • PMGKRA creates employment by engaging in various rural development projects, beneficiaries can earn wages and support their livelihoods.

  • PMGKRA helps alleviate financial distress and ensures income stability for rural households.

  • Beneficiaries of PMGKRA may receive training and skill development opportunities as part of their participation in rural development projects.

  • PMGKRA stimulates economic growth in rural areas by generating employment opportunities and investing in infrastructure development.

Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana


  • The Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY) was introduced by the Government of India on April 8, 2015.

  • PMMY aims to provide financial support to small and micro-enterprises in India by facilitating access to credit from financial institutions.

  • PMMY aims to promote financial inclusion by providing access to credit for small and micro-enterprises that often face difficulties in obtaining loans from traditional banking channels

Eligibility


  • Individuals who are engaged in non-farm income-generating activities such as manufacturing, trading, and services are eligible.

  • Both new and existing entrepreneurs seeking financial assistance for business activities are eligible to apply.

  • While a strong credit history may enhance the borrower's eligibility,

  • Borrowers should comply with applicable legal requirements, including business registrations, licenses, and permits, as per the regulatory framework governing their business activities.

Benefits


  • PMMY facilitates the growth of these enterprises by providing funding for business activities, thereby generating employment opportunities and contributing to poverty reduction and inclusive growth.

  • PMMY stimulates economic growth by fostering the development of small and micro-enterprises, which are vital contributors to GDP growth and overall economic prosperity.

  • PMMY promotes financial inclusion by extending credit facilities to individuals from underserved and marginalized sections of society, including women, Scheduled Castes (SCs), Scheduled Tribes (STs), and other economically weaker sections.

  • PMMY promotes the empowerment of women entrepreneurs by offering special incentives and support for women-led enterprises.

Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY)


  • The Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY) was launched by the Government of India on July 15, 2015

  • The Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY) aims to provide skill development training to Indian youth across various sectors to enhance their employability and productivity.

  • PMKVY aims to impart industry-relevant skill training to unemployed and underemployed youth to make them job-ready and enhance their employability in various sectors of the economy.

  • The scheme focuses on equipping individuals with market-driven skills that are in demand by industries.

Eligibility


  • Typically, individuals between the ages of 15 to 59 years are eligible to participate in PMKVY training programs.

  • PMKVY prioritizes certain target groups, including unemployed youth, school/college dropouts, economically disadvantaged individuals, women, minorities, and differently-abled persons

  • Participants are typically required to be residents of India to be eligible for PMKVY training programs.

  • Individuals with a criminal record or those involved in illegal activities may be disqualified from participating in PMKVY training programs.

Benefits


  • PMKVY provides skill training in various sectors, equipping participants with industry-relevant skills and certifications.

  • Participants receive hands-on training, practical experience, and exposure to modern techniques and technologies, ensuring they are well-prepared for employment or entrepreneurship.

  • PMKVY offers financial assistance to participants in the form of training subsidies, assessment fees, and certification costs.

  • PMKVY recognizes prior learning and provides certification to individuals based on their existing skills and experience.

National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS)


  • The National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS) was introduced by the Government of India on August 19, 2016.

  • National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS) aims to promote apprenticeship training in India by incentivizing employers to engage in apprenticeship programs and by providing skill training to youth for better employability.

  • The scheme is part of the government's efforts to address the skill gap, enhance employment opportunities, and foster industry-academia collaboration.

Eligibility


  • Typically, individuals aged 14 years or older may be eligible to participate in apprenticeship programs.

  • Candidates should demonstrate aptitude and interest in the chosen trade or occupation for which the apprenticeship is being offered.

  • Apprentices should have legal authorization to work in India, as per the applicable laws and regulations.

  • candidates are required to register themselves on the National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS) portal or any other designated apprenticeship portal to be eligible for apprenticeship opportunities

Benefits


  • Apprenticeships provide hands-on training and practical experience in a real work environment, allowing apprentices to develop industry-specific skills and competencies that are in demand by employers.

  • Completing an apprenticeship program increases the likelihood of securing employment, as apprentices gain valuable work experience and industry-recognized certifications during their training period.

  • Apprentices receive a stipend or wage from their employers during the training period, allowing them to earn a living while acquiring new skills and knowledge.
