Agriculture


PM-KISAN: Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi:


  • Established Year: 2019

  • PM-KISAN provides direct income support to small and marginal farmers by transferring ₹6,000 per year in three equal installments directly into their bank accounts.

  • The scheme aims to ensure financial assistance to farmers for meeting their agricultural expenses and improving their income security.

  • The scheme also aims to prevent gender-based discrimination and violence against women.

Eligibility


  • Small and marginal farmers across India.

  • Farmers holding cultivable land as per the land records of the respective States/UTs.

  • Families where at least one member is engaged in farming.

  • Beneficiaries should possess a valid Aadhaar number.

Benefits


  • Direct income support of ₹6,000 per year in three installments.

  • Enhances income security and financial assistance for agricultural expenses.

  • Supports over 14 crore farmers across India.

  • Improves livelihoods and purchasing power in rural areas.

PMFBY: Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana:


  • Established Year: 2016

  • PMFBY is a crop insurance scheme that provides financial protection to farmers against crop loss due to natural calamities, pests, and diseases.

  • It aims to mitigate the financial risks associated with farming and promote agricultural resilience.

Eligibility


  • All farmers across India involved in growing notified crops.

  • Loans availed for the notified crops are covered under compulsory coverage.

  • Jointly operated bank accounts for loans availed by the tenant farmers.

  • Farmers should register themselves and provide accurate crop details

Benefits


  • Financial protection against crop loss due to natural calamities, pests, and diseases.

  • Ensures compensation for crop losses due to adverse weather conditions.

  • Encourages farmers to invest in better agricultural practices without fear of financial loss.

  • Provides a safety net for farmers, contributing to agricultural resilience and stability.

PM-AASHA: Pradhan Mantri Annadata Aay Sanrakshan Abhiyan:


  • Established Year: 2018

  • PM-AASHA aims to provide remunerative prices to farmers for their produce through procurement, price support, and market intervention.

  • Protects farmers' income security and promotes market efficiency.

Eligibility


  • Farmers growing eligible crops such as pulses, oilseeds, and copra.

  • Participation in government procurement programs.

  • Compliance with quality standards and regulations.

  • Registration with designated procurement agencies..

Benefits


  • Ensures fair prices and income security for farmers.

  • Stabilizes prices and mitigates market risks.

  • Promotes agricultural sustainability and market efficiency

  • Contributes to food security and rural prosperity.

PMKSY: Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana


  • Established Year: 2015

  • PMKSY aims to promote water conservation, efficient water use, and sustainable irrigation practices in agriculture.

  • The scheme focuses on enhancing water availability, improving irrigation infrastructure, and increasing water use efficiency to boost agricultural productivity and reduce water scarcity.

Eligibility


  • Farmers, agricultural landowners, and water users' associations.

  • Landholding farmers with a minimum area of land as per the scheme guidelines

  • Participation in water management and conservation activities

  • Compliance with scheme guidelines and local water use regulations.

Benefits


  • Increases irrigated area, crop productivity, and farm income through improved water management practices.

  • Reduces water wastage and enhances soil moisture retention, mitigating drought risks.

  • Promotes sustainable water use, environmental conservation, and resilience to climate change.

  • Enhances agricultural productivity and reduces water scarcity in water-stressed regions.

PM-SYM: Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maan-dhan Yojana:


  • Established Year: 2019

  • PM-SYM is a voluntary pension scheme for unorganized sector workers, including farmers.

  • It provides a minimum assured pension of ₹3,000 per month to beneficiaries between the ages of 18 and 40 years upon reaching the age of 60.

Eligibility


  • Unorganized sector workers, including farmers, between the ages of 18 and 40 years.

  • Monthly income not exceeding ₹15,000.

  • Not covered under any statutory social security scheme.

  • Possession of a savings bank account and Aadhaar number.

Benefits


  • Provides a minimum assured pension of ₹3,000 per month upon reaching the age of 60.

  • Offers financial security and pension benefits to unorganized sector workers, including farmers, during old age.

  • Ensures well-being and dignity in retirement, enhancing social security for vulnerable groups.

  • Encourages voluntary participation and savings among unorganized sector workers for their future.

National Agricultural Market (e-NAM) Scheme:


  • Established Year: 2016

  • e-NAM is a pan-India electronic trading platform for agricultural commodities aimed at creating a unified national market for farmers and traders.

  • It facilitates online trading of agricultural produce, enables transparent price discovery, and reduces intermediaries' role in the agricultural marketing chain.

  • The scheme aims to promote farmers' welfare, enhance market access, and improve price realization for agricultural produce.

Eligibility


  • Farmers, traders, and commission agents registered with APMCs.

  • Valid PAN card for traders participating in electronic trading..

  • AAccess to internet and digital platforms for online trading

  • Compliance with quality standards and regulations

Benefits


  • Provides wider market access and better price discovery.

  • Reduces transaction costs and post-harvest losses.

  • Promotes fair trade practices and market efficiency.

  • Enhances farmers' income and livelihood security.

RKVY: Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana:


  • Established Year: 2007

  • Supports agricultural development projects, technology adoption, and capacity building.

  • Promotes holistic development of agriculture and allied sectors at the state level.

Eligibility


  • All states and union territories in India.

  • States formulating perspective plans aligning with national priorities.

  • Utilization of funds for approved agricultural projects.

  • Compliance with scheme guidelines and regulations.

Benefits


  • Provides financial assistance for agricultural development.

  • Enhances agricultural productivity and income of farmers

  • Promotes overall rural development and prosperity.
