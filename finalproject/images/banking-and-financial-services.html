PMJDY: Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana:


  • Established Year: 2014

  • Aims to provide universal access to banking facilities.

  • Encourages financial inclusion and access to basic banking services.

  • Offers benefits such as zero-balance accounts, insurance, and overdraft facilities.

Eligibility


  • Indian citizens above the age of 10.

  • No mandatory requirement for minimum balance.

  • Valid identity proof such as Aadhaar card, passport, or voter ID.

  • Address proof documents like Aadhaar card, passport, or utility bills.

Benefits


  • Enables access to banking services for unbanked households.

  • Facilitates direct benefit transfers and subsidies from the government.

  • Provides insurance coverage and overdraft facilities.

  • Promotes financial literacy and empowerment among low-income groups.

PMMY: Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana:


  • Established Year: 2015

  • Facilitates collateral-free loans to micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs).

  • Supports entrepreneurship and small business development.

Eligibility


  • Micro and small enterprises including entrepreneurs, vendors, and artisans.

  • Loan requirements up to ₹10 lakh for income-generating activities.

  • No requirement for collateral security for loans under the scheme.

  • Registered or unregistered business entities in manufacturing, trading, or service sectors.

Benefits


  • Provides access to finance for small businesses and startups.

  • Promotes entrepreneurship and job creation.

  • Enables expansion and growth of MSMEs.

  • Supports economic development and self-employment opportunities..

PMJJBY: Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana


  • Established Year: 2015

  • Provides financial security to the insured person's family in case of death.

  • Offers affordable life insurance coverage to individuals.

Eligibility


  • Indian citizens aged between 18 and 50 years.

  • Should have a savings bank account.

  • Opt-in for the scheme by providing consent and paying the premium.

  • No requirement for medical examination or health checks.

Benefits


  • Provides financial assistance to the nominee in case of the insured's death.

  • Affordable premium with easy enrollment process.

  • Offers life coverage with no requirement for health checks.

  • Supports financial security and stability for families.

Atal Pension Yojana (APY):


  • Established Year: 2015

  • Provides a pension scheme for unorganized sector workers.

  • Offers guaranteed pension ranging from ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 per month upon retirement.

  • Aims to ensure social security and financial stability for workers in their old age.

Eligibility


  • Indian citizens aged between 18 and 40 years.

  • Possession of a savings bank account.

  • Regular contributions to the scheme until the age of 60.

  • No previous pension coverage under any statutory social security scheme.

Benefits


  • Offers a guaranteed pension amount based on the contribution level.

  • Provides financial security and stability during old age.

  • Enables workers to accumulate savings for retirement.

  • Encourages voluntary participation and savings for retirement.

National Pension System (NPS):


  • Established Year: 2004

  • Offers a voluntary, contributory pension system for all citizens.

  • Provides retirement income through systematic investments in pension funds.

  • Aims to provide financial security and stability for individuals during retirement.

Eligibility


  • Indian citizens aged between 18 and 65 years.

  • Open to both salaried and self-employed individuals.

  • Requires a Permanent Retirement Account Number (PRAN) for registration.

  • Contributions can be made regularly or as a lump sum.

Benefits


  • Enables individuals to accumulate savings for retirement.

  • Offers a choice of investment options and pension fund managers.

  • Provides flexibility in contributions and withdrawals.

  • EOffers tax benefits under Section 80CCD of the Income Tax Act.

Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY):


  • Established Year: 2015

  • Offers an affordable personal accident insurance scheme for all citizens.

  • Provides coverage against accidental death and disability.

  • Aims to provide financial security and support to individuals and their families in case of accidental injuries.

Eligibility


  • Indian citizens aged between 18 and 70 years.

  • Possession of a savings bank account linked to Aadhaar.

  • Enrollment through submission of a simple application form and payment of premium.

  • No health check-up required for enrollment.

Benefits


  • Offers a lump sum payment to the nominee in case of accidental death or disability

  • Provides financial support for medical expenses and rehabilitation in case of disability.

  • Affordable premium of ₹12 per annum for coverage of ₹2 lakh for accidental death and permanent disability.
