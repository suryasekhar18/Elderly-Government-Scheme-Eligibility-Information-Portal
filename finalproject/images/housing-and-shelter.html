Housing and Shelter Services


Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY):


  • Established Year: 2015

  • Aims to provide affordable housing to all urban and rural households by 2022.

  • Offers financial assistance and subsidies for the construction, purchase, or renovation of homes.

  • Addresses housing shortages and improves living conditions for economically weaker sections, low-income groups, and middle-income groups.

Eligibility


  • Urban and rural households without pucca houses.

  • Economically weaker sections (EWS), low-income groups (LIG), and middle-income groups (MIG) based on income criteria.

  • Individuals or families meeting the scheme's beneficiary selection criteria.

  • Possession of Aadhaar card and other required documents for verification.

Benefits


  • Offers financial assistance in the form of interest subsidies and credit-linked subsidies.

  • Facilitates the construction or purchase of affordable homes with modern amenities.

  • Improves housing infrastructure and living standards for beneficiaries.

  • Promotes inclusive urban development and addresses housing challenges in both urban and rural areas.

Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS):


  • Established Year: 2015

  • A component of the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY).

  • Offers interest subsidies on home loans to eligible beneficiaries belonging to EWS, LIG, and MIG categories.

  • Aims to make housing more affordable and accessible to economically weaker sections and low-income groups.

Eligibility


  • Individuals or families belonging to EWS, LIG, and MIG income categories.

  • Home loans availed for the purchase or construction of a house.

  • Fulfillment of income criteria specified under the scheme for each category.

  • Compliance with CLSS guidelines and documentation requirements.

Benefits


  • Provides interest subsidies ranging from 3% to 6.5% on home loans for eligible beneficiaries.

  • Reduces the burden of home loan EMIs and makes housing more affordable.

  • Encourages homeownership and promotes investment in real estate.

  • AFacilitates the construction of affordable homes and addresses housing shortages.

Rajiv Awas Yojana (RAY):


  • Established Year: 2011

  • Aims to provide affordable housing to slum dwellers and urban poor.

  • Focuses on slum rehabilitation, in-situ redevelopment, and creation of affordable housing stock.

  • Seeks to create a slum-free India by providing basic amenities and housing to all urban poor households.

Eligibility


  • Slum dwellers living in notified slums identified for redevelopment.

  • Urban poor households without access to adequate housing and basic amenities.

  • OBeneficiaries identified through socio-economic surveys conducted by local authorities.

  • Compliance with scheme guidelines and beneficiary selection criteria.

Benefits


  • Facilitates slum redevelopment and creation of affordable housing units.

  • Provides basic amenities like water supply, sanitation, and electricity to slum dwellers.

  • Improves living conditions and socio-economic status of urban poor households.

  • Promotes inclusive urban development and addresses housing challenges in urban areas.

Housing for All (HFA) Mission:


  • Established Year: 2015

  • Aims to ensure housing for all urban households by 2022.

  • Focuses on affordable housing construction, slum rehabilitation, and promoting affordable rental housing.

  • Seeks to address the housing needs of economically weaker sections, low-income groups, and middle-income groups in urban areas.

Eligibility


  • Urban households without pucca houses or living in kutcha houses.

  • Economically weaker sections (EWS), low-income groups (LIG), and middle-income groups (MIG) based on income criteria.

  • Beneficiaries identified through socio-economic surveys and housing demand assessments.

  • Possession of Aadhaar card and other required documents for verification.

Benefits


  • Provides financial assistance, interest subsidies, and credit-linked subsidies for housing construction.

  • Facilitates the construction of affordable homes with basic amenities and infrastructure.

  • Improves living standards and quality of life for urban households.

  • Promotes sustainable urban development and inclusive growth in cities and towns..

Integrated Housing and Slum Development Programme (IHSDP):


  • Established Year: 2005

  • Aims to improve the living conditions of slum dwellers through housing and infrastructure development.

  • Focuses on providing basic amenities, tenure security, and social infrastructure in slums.

  • Seeks to promote slum-free cities and inclusive urban development.

Eligibility


  • Slum dwellers living in notified slums identified for redevelopment.

  • rban local bodies and municipalities implementing slum redevelopment projects

  • Beneficiaries identified through socio-economic surveys conducted by local authorities.

  • Compliance with scheme guidelines and beneficiary selection criteria..

Benefits


  • Facilitates the construction of pucca houses with basic amenities for slum dwellers.

  • Provides access to water supply, sanitation, electricity, and community facilities in slums.

  • Improves living standards and quality of life for slum residents.

  • Promotes inclusive urban development and poverty alleviation efforts.

Swarna Jayanti Shahari Rozgar Yojana (SJSRY):


  • Established Year: 1997

  • Aims to provide gainful employment and housing to urban poor households.

  • Focuses on skill development, self-employment, and housing construction for urban poor.

  • Seeks to alleviate poverty and promote sustainable livelihoods in urban areas.

Eligibility


  • Urban poor households with low incomes and limited access to employment opportunities.

  • Individuals or groups interested in skill development, self-employment, or housing construction

  • Beneficiaries identified through socio-economic surveys conducted by local authorities.

  • Compliance with scheme guidelines and beneficiary selection criteria.

Benefits


  • Offers skill training, credit facilities, and entrepreneurial support to urban poor individuals

  • Offers a choice of investment options and pension fund managers.

  • Provides financial assistance and subsidies for housing construction or improvement.

  • Promotes economic empowerment and social inclusion among urban poor communities..

Jawaharlal Nehru National Urban Renewal Mission (JNNURM):


  • Established Year: 2005

  • Aims to promote sustainable and inclusive urban development across India.

  • Focuses on infrastructure development, housing, and poverty alleviation in urban areas.

  • Seeks to improve the quality of life for urban residents through targeted interventions and urban reforms

Eligibility


  • Urban local bodies (ULBs), municipalities, and state governments are eligible for funding and support under the mission.

  • Projects identified for infrastructure development, housing, and urban renewal in eligible cities and towns.

  • Compliance with JNNURM guidelines and project criteria set by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs.

  • Beneficiaries of housing projects include economically weaker sections (EWS), low-income groups (LIG), and slum dwellers.

Benefits


  • Facilitates capacity building, technical assistance, and institutional reforms in urban governance and planning./li>
  • Provides financial support for medical expenses and rehabilitation in case of disability.

  • Promotes inclusive development by addressing the needs of vulnerable populations, including the urban poor and slum dwellers.
