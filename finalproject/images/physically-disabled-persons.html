EmpowerAbility: Schemes for Persons with Disabilities


Accessible India Campaign (Sugamya Bharat Abhiyan)


  • Sugamya Bharat Abhiyan was Launched in 2015

  • This campaign aims to make buildings, transportation, and information accessible to PwDs

  • Thus fostering inclusivity and equal opportunities in Indian society.

  • It addresses barriers faced by persons with disabilities in physical infrastructure and digital platforms, aiming to create a more inclusive and accessible environment nationwide.

Eligibility


  • Generally, individuals must possess a valid disability certificate issued by the appropriate medical authority

  • The disability percentage may vary depending on the scheme.

  • pplicants may need to be residents of India to be eligible for certain schemes.

  • Applicants may be required to provide supporting documents such as identity proof, address proof, and medical certificates as per the requirements of the scheme.

Benefits


  • The campaign aligns with the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016, which mandates equal rights and opportunities for persons with disabilities. Compliance with accessibility standards ensures that public and private entities fulfill their legal obligations.

  • This includes making ramps, accessible toilets, and other facilities available in public places to ensure inclusivity.

  • By enhancing accessibility, the campaign aims to empower persons with disabilities by providing them with equal opportunities to participate in various aspects of life, including education, employment, and social activities.

  • Making infrastructure and services accessible not only benefits persons with disabilities but also contributes to the overall development of society by fostering inclusivity and diversity.

National Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation (NHFDC) Scheme:


  • The National Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation (NHFDC) Scheme was launched on January 24, 1997

  • It was established by the Indian government with the objective of promoting economic development among Persons with Disabilities (PwD) by providing them with financial assistance

  • It also offers loans at concessional rates to PwD for self-employment ventures.

Eligibility


  • Applicants must possess a valid disability certificate issued by a recognized medical authority.

  • Applicants must be citizens of India.

  • The age limit may vary depending on the scheme, but generally, individuals above a certain age (usually 18 years) are eligible to apply.

  • r schemes related to self-employment or entrepreneurship, applicants may need to submit a detailed business plan or project proposal.

  • Applicants are usually required to have a bank account to receive financial assistance.

Benefits


  • NHFDC offers various loan schemes at concessional interest rates to PWDs for starting their own businesses, self-employment ventures, purchasing equipment, or for any other productive purposes.

  • NHFDC provides financial assistance for skill development training programs tailored for PWDs to enhance their employability and entrepreneurship skills.

  • NHFDC offers scholarships and grants for pursuing higher education, technical and professional courses, enhancing the educational opportunities for PWDs.

  • : The corporation facilitates the purchase of assistive devices and aids like wheelchairs, hearing aids, prosthetic devices, etc., at subsidized rates, thereby improving the quality of life for PWDs.

Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana (RVY)


  • TThe Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana (RVY) was launched by the Government of India on April 1, 2017.

  • Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana aims to improve their mobility, independence, and overall well-being, enabling them to lead more active and dignified lives in their later years.

  • The scheme includes awareness and outreach activities to inform eligible senior citizens and their families about the benefits of assistive devices and how to avail them under the Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana.

Eligibility


  • Beneficiaries must be senior citizens aged 60 years and above.

  • Beneficiaries should belong to Below Poverty Line (BPL) families. This criterion ensures that the scheme targets those who may not have the financial means to procure assistive devices on their own.

  • Beneficiaries should have age-related disabilities or impairments that affect their mobility, communication, or other aspects of daily life.

  • The scheme primarily aims to assist senior citizens with disabilities who require assistive devices for better quality of life.

Benefits


  • Eligible senior citizens receive assistive devices such as walking sticks, elbow crutches, walkers, hearing aids, wheelchairs, artificial dentures, and spectacles free of cost.

  • These devices help enhance mobility, communication, and overall quality of life for senior citizens with disabilities.

  • The provision of free assistive devices alleviates the financial burden on senior citizens and their families who may struggle to afford such equipment due to economic constraints.

  • RVY promotes the dignity and empowerment of senior citizens with disabilities by addressing their specific needs and enabling them to lead more independent and fulfilling lives.

Deendayal Disabled Rehabilitation Scheme (DDRS)


  • The Deendayal Disabled Rehabilitation Scheme (DDRS) was launched by the Government of India in 1999.

  • It was initiated by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment with the aim of providing comprehensive rehabilitation services to persons with disabilities (PWDs) in India.

  • DDRS focuses on rehabilitation services for persons with disabilities, including early intervention, education, vocational training, community-based rehabilitation, and creation of barrier-free environments.

Eligibility


  • ndividuals applying for assistance under DDRS must have a recognized disability as per the definitions and criteria outlined by the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 (RPwD Act).

  • Typically, the scheme is available to residents of India, and beneficiaries must provide proof of residency to avail of the services or benefits under DDRS.

  • Some components of DDRS may have specific age criteria, especially those related to services for children with disabilities or elderly individuals

  • For instance, early intervention services may be available for children up to a certain age, while vocational training programs may target youth or adults with disabilities.

Benefits


  • DDRS provides a wide range of rehabilitation services tailored to the diverse needs of persons with disabilities.

  • DDRS facilitates access to education for children with disabilities by providing special education services, assistive devices, and support for inclusive education initiatives.

  • This helps ensure that children with disabilities have equal opportunities to learn and succeed academically.

  • DDRS offers vocational training programs and skill development initiatives to enhance the employability and economic independence of PWDs.

Scheme for Implementation of Persons with Disabilities Act (SIPDA)


  • Scheme for Implementation of Persons with Disabilities Act (SIPDA),1995

  • SIPDA focuses on the effective implementation of the Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation)

  • It includes measures for creating barrier-free environments, ensuring accessibility, and promoting the rights and welfare of PWDs.

Eligibility


  • Individuals seeking assistance or benefits under SIPDA-related schemes must have a recognized disability as per the definitions and criteria outlined in the Persons with Disabilities Act, 1995

  • Typically, beneficiaries must be residents of India to avail of the services, programs, or benefits under SIPDA-related initiatives.

  • Depending on the nature of the assistance or support being provided, there may be specific eligibility criteria related to the type or severity of the disability.

  • Some components of SIPDA-related schemes may have income criteria, particularly for financial assistance or benefits. These criteria may target economically disadvantaged individuals or families among persons with disabilities.

Benefits


  • SIPDA helps in safeguarding the rights of persons with disabilities by promoting the implementation of legal provisions aimed at ensuring equal opportunities, protection against discrimination, and full participation in society.

  • SIPDA initiatives often include measures to enhance accessibility in public spaces, buildings, transportation, and information and communication technology (ICT) infrastructure.

  • SIPDA initiatives often include awareness campaigns, sensitization programs, and capacity-building activities aimed at promoting a better understanding of disability issues, reducing stigma and discrimination, and fostering a culture of inclusion and acceptance in society.

  • This benefits PWDs by making physical and digital environments more inclusive and accessible.

Inclusive Education for Disabled at Secondary Stage (IEDSS)


  • The Inclusive Education for Disabled at Secondary Stage (IEDSS) was launched in India in 2009

  • It aimed to provide educational opportunities for students with disabilities at the secondary level (classes IX to XII) in regular schools

  • IEDSS aims to ensure that students with disabilities have equal access to secondary education, removing barriers that may prevent them from attending regular schools.

  • IEDSS fosters an inclusive environment where students with disabilities can learn alongside their peers without disabilities. This promotes social interaction, understanding, and acceptance among all students.

Eligibility


  • Students must provide documentation or certification of their disability from a recognized medical or educational authority.

  • The disability may include physical, sensory, intellectual, or developmental impairments.

  • The program usually targets students of secondary school age, typically between the ages of 14 to 18 or equivalent grades, depending on the educational system in place.

  • Eligible students must demonstrate a need for additional support or accommodations to participate effectively in secondary education due to their disability.

Benefits


  • IEDSS ensures that students with disabilities have equal access to quality secondary education, empowering them to pursue their academic and personal goals alongside their peers.

  • By providing individualized support and accommodations, IEDSS helps students with disabilities overcome barriers to learning, leading to improved academic performance and outcomes.

  • Inclusive education promotes social interaction and understanding among students with and without disabilities, fostering empathy, acceptance, and inclusion in school

  • Inclusive education provides opportunities for students with disabilities to develop essential life skills such as communication, problem-solving, and self-advocacy, which are crucial for their future independence and success.

Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme


  • Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme was introduced in India in the year 2009.

  • It was launched by the Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India, with the aim of providing financial assistance to persons with disabilities who are living below the poverty line.

  • This scheme provides monthly income support to economically disadvantaged adults living with disabilities.

Eligibility


  • Applicant age between 18-79 years.

  • Must be a resident of India.

  • Physically or mentally disabled

  • Disability severity over 80%.

  • Dwarfs eligible.

  • Below Poverty Line (BPL) status required.

Benefits


  • The scheme provides a monthly pension to eligible persons with disabilities, helping them meet their basic living expenses, including food, clothing, and shelter.

  • IGNDPS targets individuals with disabilities who are living below the poverty line, thereby helping to alleviate poverty among this vulnerable population.

  • By providing financial assistance, IGNDPS empowers persons with disabilities to lead more independent and dignified lives, enabling them to participate more fully in society.

  • By offering financial assistance, the scheme recognizes the rights of persons with disabilities to live with dignity and equality, as enshrined in various national and international laws and conventions.
