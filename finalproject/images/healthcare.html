Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)


  • The Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) was introduced in India by the Government of India on May 9, 2015.

  • It was launched along with two other social security schemes: the Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY) and the Atal Pension Yojana (APY)

  • PMSBY is an accidental insurance scheme aimed at providing affordable personal accident insurance to people from all walks of life.

  • The primary objective is to Alleviate the burden of medical and rehabilitation expenses for accident victims and their families.

Eligibility


  • Individuals between the ages of 18 years and 70 years are generally eligible to enroll in the scheme.

  • However, the specific age range may vary depending on the guidelines set by the implementing authority.

  • The applicant must have an active savings bank account. Often, the scheme is offered through participating banks, and applicants are required to have a bank account with one of these banks to enroll.

  • Applicants must provide consent for auto-debit of the premium amount from their bank account.

  • Individuals interested in availing of the scheme need to enroll and pay the annual premium through their bank account.

Benefits


  • In the unfortunate event of the insured's death due to an accident, a lump sum amount is payable to the nominee or legal heir as per the terms of the policy.

  • If the insured suffers permanent total disability due to an accident, a predetermined sum assured is paid out to support the insured's ongoing medical treatment and living expenses.

  • In case of partial disability resulting from an accident, a specified percentage of the sum assured is paid to the insured to assist with medical expenses and loss of income..

  • The financial support provided by PMSBY in the event of accidental death or disability can help alleviate the financial burden on the insured's family and dependents.

  • The coverage under PMSBY is typically renewable on an annual basis, allowing individuals to continue benefiting from the scheme's protection year after year.

Ayushman Bharat


  • Ayushman Bharat Introduced in India on September 23, 2018.

  • Ayushman Bharat, also known as the Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PMJAY)

  • It was launched by the Government of India as the world's largest government-funded healthcare scheme.

  • Aimed at providing health insurance coverage to over 500 million economically vulnerable citizens across the country.

Eligibility


  • There is no age limit for beneficiaries under the scheme. All members of eligible households, irrespective of age, can avail of the benefits.

  • The scheme covers all members of eligible families, regardless of family size. There is no upper limit on the number of beneficiaries per family.

  • Both urban and rural households meeting the specified criteria are eligible for coverage under AB-PMJAY.

  • Families identified as per the SECC database with specific deprivation criteria such as households without shelter, landless households, households with only one room with kutcha walls and kutcha roof, and families with no adult members between the ages of 16 and 59.

Benefits


  • AB-PMJAY provides health insurance coverage of up to Rs. 5 lakh per eligible family per year, covering the costs of medical treatment for various illnesses and procedures.

  • eneficiaries can avail of cashless treatment at empaneled public and private hospitals across India for listed medical procedures and treatments.

  • The scheme covers a wide range of medical conditions, including both pre-existing and new illnesses, ensuring that beneficiaries have access to essential healthcare services without financial constraints.

  • The scheme offers portability, allowing beneficiaries to avail of treatment at any empaneled hospital within their state or across India, providing flexibility and convenience in accessing healthcare services.

  • AB-PMJAY aims to improve access to quality healthcare services for economically vulnerable families, ensuring that they receive timely and appropriate medical treatment.

National Health Mission (NHM)


  • NHM was launched in 2013

  • It address health inequalities and strengthen healthcare delivery systems in India.

  • It encompasses various programs aimed at improving maternal and child health, reducing communicable and non-communicable diseases, and providing universal access to healthcare services

  • NHM aims to address health inequalities, strengthen healthcare delivery systems, and improve health outcomes across rural and urban areas of India.

Eligibility


  • ligibilities for NHM programs can vary depending on the specific scheme or role you're interested in.

  • There may be an age limit for certain positions within NHM programs. This can vary based on the position and the state or region in which the program is being implemented.

  • NHM programs may prioritize candidates who are residents of the specific area or region where the program is being implemented. This is often the case for community health worker roles.

Benefits


  • Some NHM programs provide health insurance coverage for employees and their dependents. This can include coverage for medical expenses, hospitalization, and other healthcare needs.

  • NHM focuses on improving maternal and child health outcomes by providing antenatal care, postnatal care, institutional deliveries, immunizations, nutritional support, and interventions to reduce infant and maternal mortality rates.

  • NHM implements various disease control and prevention programs to combat diseases such as malaria, tuberculosis, HIV/AIDS, vector-borne diseases, and waterborne diseases, thereby improving public health outcomes.

  • NHM recruits and trains healthcare professionals, including doctors, nurses, paramedics, and community health workers, to address the shortage of healthcare workers and improve the delivery of healthcare services across the country.

Rashtriya Swasthya Bima Yojana (RSBY)


  • The Rashtriya Swasthya Bima Yojana (RSBY) is a government-run health insurance scheme in India launched in 2008.

  • It aims to provide health insurance coverage to below-poverty-line (BPL) families and other identified vulnerable groups.

  • Rashtriya Swasthya Bima Yojana plays a crucial role in expanding health insurance coverage and improving access to healthcare services for marginalized and economically disadvantaged populations in India

  • It provides financial protection against healthcare expenses and helps alleviate the burden of medical costs on vulnerable families.

Eligibility


  • RSBY is primarily aimed at providing health insurance coverage to families living below the poverty line. Therefore, households identified as BPL are usually eligible to enroll in the scheme.

  • Beneficiaries need to have a valid proof of identity (such as Aadhaar card, voter ID card, ration card, etc.) to enroll in RSBY.

  • Certain occupational categories such as manual scavengers, street vendors, construction workers, domestic workers, etc., may also be eligible for RSBY coverage, even if they are not officially categorized as BPL.

  • RSBY is implemented at the state level in India. Therefore, eligibility criteria may vary slightly from state to state, depending on the specific guidelines issued by the respective state governments.

Benefits


  • RSBY provides health insurance coverage to BPL families, offering financial protection against the cost of hospitalization and certain medical treatments.

  • Under RSBY, beneficiaries can avail of cashless treatment in empanelled hospitals. This means that they can receive medical care without having to pay cash upfront, as the insurance covers the expenses directly.

  • scheme covers the beneficiary and up to five family members, providing a comprehensive health insurance solution for eligible households.

  • RSBY offers portability, allowing beneficiaries to avail of treatment in any empanelled hospital across India, irrespective of their location.

  • The premium for RSBY is highly subsidized by the government, making it affordable for BPL families to access quality healthcare services.

Pradhan Mantri Surakshit Matritva Abhiyan (PMSMA)


  • Pradhan Mantri Surakshit Matritva Abhiyan (PMSMA) was introduced by the Government of India on June 9, 2016.

  • It was launched as an initiative to provide comprehensive antenatal care to pregnant women, especially those in rural and underserved areas, with the aim of reducing maternal and infant mortality rates.

  • The main objective of the Pradhan Mantri Surakshit Matritva Abhiyan (PMSMA) is to provide comprehensive antenatal care to pregnant women, particularly those in rural and underserved areas, to ensure their well-being and that of their unborn child.

Eligibility


  • Pregnant women residing in India are eligible for PMSMA. There may not be specific restrictions based on state or region, as the program is intended to benefit pregnant women across the country.

  • PMSMA is typically targeted towards women from economically disadvantaged backgrounds. However, there may not be strict income-based eligibility criteria

  • Women must be pregnant to avail of the benefits under the PMSMA. This includes women in both rural and urban areas.

  • Pregnant women may need to register for the PMSMA at designated healthcare facilities or through outreach programs organized by the government.

Benefits


  • Pregnant women enrolled in PMSMA receive comprehensive antenatal care services, including regular health check-ups, screening for pregnancy-related complications, and access to essential maternal and child health interventions.

  • The program focuses on identifying high-risk pregnancies early on through specialized antenatal check-ups, enabling healthcare providers to monitor the health status of both the mother and the unborn child closely and intervene promptly if any complications arise.

  • PMSMA aims to reduce maternal mortality rates by addressing pregnancy-related complications and ensuring safe deliveries. Additionally, promoting institutional deliveries can help reduce infant mortality rates by improving access to skilled birth attendance and emergency obstetric care.

  • The program provides pregnant women with access to counseling and guidance on various aspects of maternal and child health, including nutrition, breastfeeding, immunization, and family planning, empowering them to make informed decisions regarding their health and the health of their babies.

Pradhan Mantri National Dialysis Programme


  • The Pradhan Mantri National Dialysis Programme was launched in India on May 25, 2016.

  • It aimed to provide free dialysis services to the poor in district hospitals across the country.

  • The primary objective of the Pradhan Mantri National Dialysis Programme in India is to provide accessible and affordable dialysis services to patients suffering from kidney failure, especially those from economically weaker sections of society.

  • Additionally, the program seeks to enhance awareness about kidney diseases and promote preventive measures to reduce the incidence of kidney failure.

  • The program aims to reduce the financial burden on patients and their families, increase the availability of dialysis machines, and improve the overall quality of care for kidney patients across the nation.

Eligibility


  • Patients belonging to economically weaker sections of society, particularly those below a certain income threshold, are often prioritized for free dialysis services under the PMNDP. The income criteria may vary from state to state or healthcare facility to healthcare facility.

  • Eligibility for the PMNDP is typically based on the patient's medical condition and need for dialysis treatment.

  • Patients must be residents of India to be eligible for the PMNDP. The program is intended to benefit Indian citizens who require dialysis services but cannot afford them.

  • Patients may need to register for the PMNDP at designated healthcare facilities and provide necessary documentation to establish their eligibility.

  • This documentation may include proof of identity, proof of residency, income certificates, and medical records indicating the need for dialysis treatment.

Benefits


  • The primary benefit of PMNDP is that it provides free dialysis treatment to eligible patients.

  • This alleviates the financial burden associated with dialysis, which is a lifelong requirement for individuals suffering from end-stage renal disease (ESRD) or other chronic kidney conditions

  • By offering free dialysis services, PMNDP ensures that economically disadvantaged patients have access to life-saving treatment without worrying about the cost.

  • This helps in improving healthcare access and reducing health disparities among different socio-economic groups.

  • Patients enrolled in PMNDP can receive regular dialysis treatment as per their medical needs. Regular dialysis sessions are essential for managing ESRD and maintaining patients' overall health and well-being.
