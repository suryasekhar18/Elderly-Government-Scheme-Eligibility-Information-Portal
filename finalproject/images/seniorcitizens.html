Atal pension yojana (APY)


  • Introduced on 9th of May, 2015

  • Atal pension yojana formerly known as Swavalamban yojana is a government-backed pension scheme in india

  • It primarrily targeted at the unoragnaised sector.It was mentioned in the year of 2015 budjet speech by finance minister Arun jaitley.

  • Its main objective is to help towards economic security of those people who become depreseed ater their working age.

  • It was funded by grants from the Government of india.

Eligibility


  • It was applicable to all citizens in the orgaines sector who joined the National pension Scheme (NPS) administrated by the Pension Fund Regulatory an Developement Authority(PRFDA) act 2013

  • The minimum eligible age of a person to join in APY is 18 years and maximum is 40 years.

  • The scheme is primarly targeted at the economically waker sections,and individuals should meet the perscribed income criteria set by governmernt.

  • The national Adhaar ID number is the primary document for identification of beneficiareis, spouses, and nominees to avoid entitltment-related diputes in the long term.

  • For proof of address, an individual may submit a copy of their ration card or bank passbook

Benefits


  • Guaranteed Pension:One of the primary benfits of APY is that it provides a guarnteed minimum pension ranging from Rs.1000 to Rs.5000 depending on the age at subscriber joined in the scheme

  • Government Contribution for Low-Income Earners:To encourage participation, the Government of India contributes 50% of the subscriber's contribution or Rs. 1,000 per year, whichever is lower, for a period of 5 years for eligible subscribers who join the scheme before December 31, 2015.

  • Flexible Contribution Levels:Subscribers have the flexibility to choose their contribution levels based on the pension amount they wish to receive upon retirement, with options ranging from Rs. 42 to Rs. 1,454 per month.

  • Easy Enrollment Process: The enrollment process for APY is simple and can be done through participating banks or through the online portal, making it accessible to a wide range of individuals.

  • Tax benfits: Contributions made towards APY are eligible for tax benefits under Section 80CCD of the Income Tax Act, providing subscribers with additional savings on their taxable income.

Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)


  • Introduced in the year of 2007

  • The old age pension scheme aims to provide social protection to the eligible beneficiaries

  • Under IGNOAP scheme, senior citizens of India will receive monthly pension.

  • Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme is a non-contribution pension. It means that the beneficiary does not have to contribute any amount to receive the pension.

Eligibility


  • Age:The primary criterion is age. Beneficiaries must usually be at least 60 years old to qualify for the pension. However, some states may have different age thresholds.

  • Income Criteria:Applicants are often required to belong to the below poverty line (BPL) category or have limited financial resources. The specific income threshold may vary from state to state.

  • Residency: Beneficiaries typically need to be residents of the state where they are applying for the pension. They may be required to provide proof of residence such as a ration card, voter ID, or Aadhaar card.

  • Exclusion from Other Pension Schemes: Individuals who already receive benefits from other pension schemes, such as the Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) or the National Pension Scheme (NPS), may not be eligible for IGNOAPS.

Benefits


  • Financial Assistance:The primary benefit of IGNOAPS is the provision of financial assistance in the form of a monthly pension to elderly individuals who are below the poverty line (BPL) or have limited financial resources

  • Poverty Alleviation: By providing a regular pension to elderly individuals who may otherwise struggle to support themselves financially, IGNOAPS contributes to poverty alleviation efforts.

  • Healthcare Access: Access to financial assistance through IGNOAPS allows elderly individuals to afford essential healthcare services, medications, and treatments.This, in turn, contributes to their overall health and well-being.

  • Social Inclusion and Empowerment: By providing financial support to elderly citizens, IGNOAPS promotes their social inclusion and empowerment. It enables them to remain active participants in society, engage in social activities.

Annapurna scheme


  • The Annapurna Scheme was introduced by the Government of India on 19th November 2000.

  • The Annapurna Scheme plays a crucial role in addressing the nutritional needs of vulnerable senior citizens who lack financial resources for their sustenance.

  • The Annapurna Scheme operates across all states and union territories of India, aiming to reach needy senior citizens in both rural and urban areas.

  • The distribution of food grains to beneficiaries is managed through designated fair price shops or distribution centers established by the state governments.

Eligibility


  • Age:Beneficiaries must be aged 65 years or above. Some states may have variations in the age criterion.

  • No Family Support:The scheme usually targets senior citizens who do not have any means of support from their families or relatives and are living in a state of deprivation

  • Residence:Beneficiaries should be residents of the respective state or union territory where the scheme is being implemented. Proof of residence may be required during the application process.

  • Exclusion from Other Schemes:Individuals who are already receiving benefits under other social security schemes, such as NOAPS or similar pension schemes, may not be eligible for the Annapurna Scheme.

Benefits


  • Food Security:The primary benefit of the Annapurna Scheme is to ensure food security for destitute senior citizens who are unable to support themselves financially. Beneficiaries receive 10 kilograms of food grains per month free of cost, typically including rice or wheat, which are staple food items in India.

  • Nutritional Support:By providing regular access to food grains, the scheme helps improve the nutritional intake of senior citizens, contributing to their overall health and well-being. Adequate nutrition is essential for maintaining good health, especially in older individuals

  • Financial Relief: For eligible senior citizens who may be struggling to meet their basic needs due to financial constraints, the provision of free food grains under the Annapurna Scheme offers significant financial relief. It helps alleviate the burden of purchasing essential food items..

  • Dignity and Respect:By ensuring access to nutritious food at no cost, the Annapurna Scheme helps preserve the dignity and self-respect of senior citizens, allowing them to live with dignity and independence in their later years.

Pradhan Mantri Vaya Vandana Yojana (PMVVY)


  • The Pradhan Mantri Vaya Vandana Yojana (PMVVY) was introduced by the Government of India on May 4, 2017.

  • It was launched to provide senior citizens with a reliable pension income to support them during their retirement years.

  • It offers them financial security and a source of stable income in their later years.

  • It provides a reliable source of pension income, helping individuals maintain their standard of living and meet their financial obligations without depending on others for support.

Eligibility


  • Age: The scheme is specifically designed for senior citizens aged 60 years and above. There is no upper age limit for participation.

  • Nationality: Applicants must be Indian citizens to be eligible for PMVVY.

  • Documentation: Applicants need to submit relevant identification documents such as Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, and proof of age while applying for PMVVY. Additionally, they need to provide bank account details for pension disbursement

  • Investment: The maximum investment allowed under PMVVY is capped at Rs. 15 lakhs per senior citizen. This investment limit applies across all policies issued to the same individual under the scheme.

Benefits


  • Guaranteed Pension Income:PMVVY provides senior citizens with a guaranteed pension income for a specified period, typically 10 years. This ensures a regular and stable source of income for the pensioners, helping them meet their financial needs during retirement.

  • Fixed Interest Rate: The interest rate offered under PMVVY is fixed for the entire duration of the policy. This protects pensioners from fluctuations in interest rates, ensuring a predictable and steady return on their investment

  • Tax Benefits:Senior citizens investing in PMVVY may be eligible for tax benefits under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, 1961. The premiums paid towards the scheme qualify for tax deductions, helping pensioners reduce their tax liabilities.


Senior Citizen Savings Scheme (SCSS)


  • The Senior Citizen Savings Scheme (SCSS) was established by the Government of India on April 2, 2004.

  • The primary objective of the SCSS is to provide senior citizens with a safe and reliable investment avenue to generate regular income during their retirement years

  • It aims to ensure financial security and stability for elderly individuals.

  • It is backed by the government, ensuring the safety of the invested capital and providing peace of mind to investors.

Eligibility


  • Age:Individuals must be at least 60 years old to open an SCSS account.

  • Additionally, individuals who have attained the age of 55 years but are below 60 years and have retired on superannuation or under a voluntary or special voluntary retirement scheme (VRS/SVRS) can also open an SCSS account, subject to certain conditions.

  • CitizenshipApplicants must be Indian citizens to be eligible for the SCSS.

  • Account Type:SCSS accounts can be opened individually or jointly with a spouse. However, only the primary account holder needs to meet the age eligibility criteria.

  • Investment Limit: The minimum investment required to open an SCSS account is Rs. 1,000, and the maximum investment limit is Rs. 15 lakhs. However, the investment amount cannot exceed the total amount received by the investor on retirement or under VRS/SVRS, whichever is lower.

Benefits


  • Regular Income: SCSS provides a reliable source of regular income for senior citizens, helping them to meet their day-to-day expenses, medical needs, and other financial requirements during their retirement years.

  • Higher Interest Rates:The interest rates offered under SCSS are generally higher compared to other savings schemes or fixed deposits offered by banks. This allows senior citizens to earn higher returns on their investments, enhancing their financial security

  • Tax Benefits:Investments made in SCSS are eligible for tax deductions under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, 1961, up to a maximum limit. This helps investors reduce their tax liabilities and increase their overall savings

  • Interest Payment Frequency:Interest on SCSS accounts is paid out quarterly, providing investors with regular income at regular intervals. This ensures a steady stream of cash flow for meeting expenses and managing finances effectively

Integrated Programme for Older Persons (IPOP)


  • The Integrated Programme for Older Persons (IPOP) was introduced in India in 1992

  • It aims to ensure their social, economic, and emotional well-being.

  • The primary goal of IPOP is to address the diverse needs of older persons in India by providing them with various support services and facilities

  • Integrated Programme for Older Persons (IPOP) reflects the government's commitment to promoting the well-being and dignity of senior citizens in India by addressing their various needs.

Eligibility


  • Age:: Generally, individuals who are 60 years old or above are eligible to benefit from programs under IPOP.

  • Residency:Usually, the individual must be a resident of the area where the program is being implemented. This may vary depending on the specific program and its jurisdiction.

  • Documentation:Applicants may be required to provide certain documents such as proof of age, residence, and any other relevant certificates.

  • Income Criteria:SCSS accounts can be opened individually or jointly with a spouse. However, only the primary account holder needs to meet the age eligibility criteria.

Benefits


  • IPOP provides access to healthcare facilities, including medical check-ups, treatment, and affordable medicines.

  • The program offers support for rehabilitation services, such as physical therapy and counseling, for older persons who may have disabilities or health challenges.

  • Eligible senior citizens may receive financial aid or pensions through IPOP, especially those who are economically vulnerable

  • IPOP conducts awareness campaigns and educational programs to raise awareness about issues related to aging, elder abuse, and the rights of older persons.
